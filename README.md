# FoodWaste Intelligence 🌱
### Simulate, Analyze, and Reduce Food Waste

## 💡 Main Idea
**FoodWaste Intelligence** is a web application designed to simulate food waste scenarios, analyze waste patterns, and explore their environmental and economic impact. The platform helps users understand why food waste occurs, investigate potential demand and waste trends, and compare different scenarios to identify opportunities for reducing food waste and financial losses.

By transforming data into meaningful insights and interactive visualizations, the project aims to support smarter, data-driven decisions and promote more sustainable food management.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Food waste leads to significant economic losses and environmental problems. Poor inventory planning, inaccurate demand estimation, and inefficient food management can contribute to unnecessary waste. Understanding these issues and evaluating possible solutions can be challenging without an accessible way to analyze different scenarios.

## 🚀 Proposed Solution
FoodWaste Intelligence provides an interactive platform where users can simulate food waste scenarios, explore trends, analyze potential losses, and compare possible outcomes. It aims to demonstrate how data analysis and predictive insights can help identify waste-reduction opportunities.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Food Waste Simulation:** Generate and explore different food waste scenarios.
- **Waste Analytics:** Analyze waste patterns and their economic impact.
- **Demand and Waste Trends:** Explore potential patterns in food demand and waste.
- **Interactive Dashboards:** Present insights through visualizations and charts.
- **Scenario Comparison:** Compare different scenarios and their potential outcomes.
- **Waste Reduction Insights:** Identify opportunities to reduce waste and financial losses.
